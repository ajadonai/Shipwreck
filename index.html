What is this?

If you're building with AI — Cursor, Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT — the code gets written fast. But fast doesn't mean safe. Your AI assistant doesn't flag missing auth, skipped error handling, or zero monitoring.

Shipwrecks is the checklist for the gaps AI leaves behind. Not theoretical. Not edge cases. The stuff that wakes you up at 3am.
